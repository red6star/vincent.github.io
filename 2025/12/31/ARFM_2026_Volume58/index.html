2. Formation and Evolution of Planetary Stagnant Lids and Crusts / 行星停滞盖层和地壳的形成与演化

Authors / 作者: Chloé Michaut¹, Anne Davaille², Stéphane Labrosse¹

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Ecole Normale Supérieure de Lyon, Université Lyon 1, CNRS, Lyon, France（法国里昂，里昂高等师范学校，里昂一大，法国国家科学研究中心）
  • ² Laboratoire FAST, CNRS, Université Paris-Saclay, Orsay, France（法国奥尔赛，FAST实验室，法国国家科学研究中心，巴黎萨克雷大学）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 1–28

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-055048

Abstract

Earth is the only known planet with plate tectonics, which involves a mobile upper thermal boundary layer. Other terrestrial planets show a one-plate immobile lithosphere, or stagnant lid, that insulates and isolates their interior. Here, we first review the different types of lids that can develop on rocky and icy bodies. As they formed by accretion, involving high-energy impacts, terrestrial planets likely started hot and molten. We examine the process of lid initiation from a magma ocean stage and develop the equations for lid growth. We survey how lateral perturbations in lid and crust thickness can be amplified during their growth and finally discuss the possible processes at the origin of lid rupture and plate generation.

摘要（中文）

地球是已知唯一存在板块构造的行星，其特征是具有一个可移动的上部热边界层。其他类地行星则表现为单板块不可移动的岩石圈，即”停滞盖层”，该盖层将其内部与外界隔绝并保温。本文首先综述了可在岩石和冰质天体上发育的不同类型盖层。由于行星通过吸积形成，涉及高能撞击事件，类地行星很可能以高温熔融状态起始。我们研究了从岩浆海洋阶段开始的盖层启动过程，并推导了盖层生长的方程。我们考察了盖层和地壳厚度中的横向扰动如何在生长过程中被放大，最后讨论了盖层破裂和板块生成的可能过程。

3. Electromagnetically Forced Flows in Shallow Electrolyte Layers / 浅层电解液中的电磁驱动流动

Authors / 作者: Sergio Cuevas¹, Sergey A. Suslov², Aldo Figueroa³

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Instituto de Energías Renovables, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México, Temixco, México（墨西哥特米斯科，墨西哥国立自治大学，可再生能源研究所）
  • ² Department of Mathematics, Swinburne University of Technology, Hawthorn, Victoria, Australia（澳大利亚维多利亚州霍桑，斯威本科技大学，数学系）
  • ³ Secihti-Centro de Investigación en Ciencias, Universidad Autónoma del Estado de Morelos, Cuernavaca, México（墨西哥库埃纳瓦卡，莫雷洛斯州自治大学，科学研究中心）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 29–53

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-051243

Abstract

Electromagnetically forced flows in shallow electrolyte layers offer a versatile and nonintrusive method for exploring quasi-two-dimensional fluid dynamics. This review focuses on the experimental and theoretical aspects of such flows driven by Lorentz forces generated by the interaction of injected electric currents and the applied magnetic fields. The method is applicable to both liquid metals and electrolytes, with the latter more commonly used due to their wide availability and ease of handling. Experimental aspects of the method and key components of mathematical flow analysis are discussed. Initially developed for geophysical flow modeling, the method has been instrumental in exploring various other physical phenomena including vortex and wake dynamics, spatiotemporal chaos, and mixing processes. The review also addresses the challenges of achieving true two-dimensionality in laboratory settings and discusses the influence of various parameters, such as layer thickness and forcing intensity, on the flow behavior. Future research directions in the field are highlighted.

摘要（中文）

浅层电解液中的电磁驱动流动提供了一种灵活且非侵入式的方法来研究准二维流体动力学。本综述聚焦于此类由洛伦兹力驱动的流动的实验和理论方面，该洛伦兹力由注入电流与外加磁场的相互作用产生。该方法适用于液态金属和电解质，后者因其广泛的可获得性和易于操作而更常使用。文章讨论了该方法的实验方面和数学流动分析的关键组成部分。该方法最初为地球物理流动模拟而开发，已在探索其他多种物理现象中发挥了重要作用，包括涡旋和尾流动力学、时空混沌以及混合过程。综述还讨论了在实验室条件下实现真正二维性的挑战，并分析了层厚和驱动力强度等参数对流动行为的影响。最后展望了该领域的未来研究方向。

4. The Dynamics of Sand Dunes / 沙丘动力学

Authors / 作者: Nathalie M. Vriend¹, Karol A. Bacik²

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Paul M. Rady Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Colorado, Boulder, Colorado, USA（美国科罗拉多州博尔德，科罗拉多大学博尔德分校，机械工程系）
  • ² Department of Mathematics, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA（美国马萨诸塞州剑桥，麻省理工学院，数学系）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 55–81

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-062843

Abstract

Sand dunes cover 5% of Earth’s land surface, and they abundantly populate river bottoms and seabeds. The subtle dynamical interplay between the granular matter and the overlaying fluid leads to rich phenomenology at different scales, from colliding grains through migrating sand dunes to slowly evolving dune fields. In this review, we survey recent developments in the literature on the dynamics of sand dunes and focus in particular on the physics and mathematics. Our discussion is organized around four central paradigms of the field: flat bed instability, single dune migration, dune–dune interactions, and dune field statistics. Besides discussing the key scientific advances, we also highlight the methodological advances in observations, experiments, and simulations that facilitated them. We conclude our review by discussing the social implications of dune dynamics, such as the interaction between dune and infrastructure, and we offer speculation on what research topics related to sand dunes might become important in the next decade.

摘要（中文）

沙丘覆盖了地球陆地表面的5%，并大量分布于河床和海底。颗粒物质与上覆流体之间微妙的动力学相互作用导致了不同尺度上的丰富现象，从颗粒碰撞到迁移的沙丘，再到缓慢演化的沙丘场。本综述回顾了沙丘动力学文献的最新进展，特别关注物理和数学方面。我们的讨论围绕该领域的四个核心范式展开：平坦床面不稳定性、单个沙丘迁移、沙丘间的相互作用以及沙丘场统计。除了讨论关键的科学进展外，我们还强调了促成这些进展的观测、实验和模拟方面的方法论进步。最后，我们讨论了沙丘动力学的社会影响（如沙丘与基础设施之间的相互作用），并对未来十年沙丘相关研究的重要方向进行了展望。

5. Secondary Atomization of Droplets at Extreme Conditions / 极端条件下的液滴二次雾化

Authors / 作者: Saini Jatin Rao¹, Saptarshi Basu¹

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Department of Mechanical Engineering, Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, India（印度班加罗尔，印度理工学院，机械工程系）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 83–110

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112823-115348

Abstract

Droplets, which are ubiquitous in nature, are formed through intriguing processes, and one such route is air-assisted atomization or aerobreakup. This review focuses on secondary atomization, particularly the breakup of an individual droplet subjected to high-speed flows. This process involves complex interfacial dynamics with multiscale deformations, ranging from global flattening to local unstable waves. The deformations occur at progressively smaller scales while interacting with the surrounding gas phase, forming a nonlinear cascade. Each local undulation serves as a precursor to a self-similar evolution or subsecondary breakup process that ends with a ligament-mediated mechanism. In practical scenarios, droplets often encounter nonuniform, unsteady, impulsive, or compressible flows, like shock waves, which pose extreme conditions. The spatiotemporal scales of the nonuniformity or unsteadiness of the external flow must be comparable with the drop deformation scales at either global or local levels to influence aerobreakup that cascades across hierarchical deformation scales. The compressible effects at high Mach numbers are interestingly shown to suppress the tendency toward breakup.

摘要（中文）

液滴在自然界中无处不在，通过多种有趣的过程形成，其中一种途径是气体辅助雾化或气动破碎。本综述聚焦于二次雾化，特别是单个液滴在高速气流作用下的破碎过程。该过程涉及复杂的界面动力学和多重尺度变形，从全局扁平化到局部不稳定波。变形在逐步减小的尺度上发生，同时与周围气相相互作用，形成非线性级联。每次局部波动都是自相似演化或亚二次破碎过程的前兆，最终以韧带介导的机制结束。在实际场景中，液滴经常遭遇非均匀、非定常、脉冲式或可压缩流动（如激波），构成极端条件。外部流动的非均匀性或非定常性的时空尺度必须与液滴变形尺度在全局或局部层面相当，才能影响跨层级变形尺度的气动破碎级联。有趣的是，高马赫数下的可压缩效应被证明会抑制破碎趋势。

6. Capillary Drainage in Horizontal Soap Films: Theoretical Review and Experimental Illustrations / 水平皂膜中的毛细排液：理论综述与实验演示

Authors / 作者: Isabelle Cantat¹,², Savinien Pertant¹, Christophe Raufaste²,³, Emmanuelle Rio⁴

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Université de Rennes, CNRS, IPR (Institut de Physique de Rennes) - UMR 6251, Rennes, France（法国雷恩，雷恩大学，法国国家科学研究中心，雷恩物理研究所）
  • ² Institut Universitaire de France, Paris, France（法国巴黎，大学研究院）
  • ³ Université Côte d’Azur, CNRS, INPHYNI, Nice, France（法国尼斯，蔚蓝海岸大学，法国国家科学研究中心，尼斯物理研究所）
  • ⁴ Université Paris-Saclay, CNRS, Laboratoire de Physique des Solides, Orsay, France（法国奥尔赛，巴黎萨克雷大学，法国国家科学研究中心，固体物理实验室）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 111–138

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-100224-111138

Abstract

Soap films and bubbles are inherently unstable systems that evolve over time. Their thickness is primarily governed by the competition between capillary and viscous forces. The presence of surfactants introduces Marangoni stresses, which limit interfacial extension and significantly increase film lifetime. While the bulk flow is typically well-described by a simple Poiseuille profile between the two interfaces, the interfacial dynamics can induce complex behaviors, even in the simple case of horizontal film drainage, which is used as a paradigmatic example in this review. The interfacial velocity is dictated by the thickness gradients and by the interfacial rheology, which, in many practical cases, reduces to the condition of an incompressible interface. This simplified framework allows for analytical predictions and scaling laws in axisymmetric flows. It is also consistent with the spontaneous symmetry breaking that may be observed in horizontal films—a phenomenon associated with the marginal regeneration process, which remains only partially understood. This review presents the most elementary theoretical frameworks capable of capturing the essential features of these flows and provides quantitative comparisons with available experimental data.

摘要（中文）

皂膜和气泡本质上是随时间演化的不稳定系统。其厚度主要由毛细力和黏性力之间的竞争决定。表面活性剂的存在引入了马兰戈尼应力，限制了界面扩展并显著延长了膜寿命。虽然体相流动通常可以很好地用两个界面之间的简单泊肃叶剖面来描述，但界面动力学即使在水平膜排液这一简单情况下（本综述将其作为范例）也会引发复杂行为。界面速度由厚度梯度和界面流变学决定，在许多实际情况中，后者可简化为不可压缩界面条件。这一简化框架允许在轴对称流动中进行分析预测和标度律推导。它也与水平膜中可能观察到的自发对称性破缺现象一致——该现象与边缘再生过程相关，目前仍只有部分被理解。本综述介绍了能够捕捉这些流动基本特征的最基本理论框架，并与现有实验数据进行了定量比较。

7. Aircraft Icing: Modeling and Simulation / 飞机结冰：建模与模拟

Authors / 作者: Alberto Guardone¹, Tommaso Bellosta¹, Alessandro Donizetti¹, Mariachiara Gallia²

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Department of Aerospace Science and Technology, Politecnico di Milano, Milano, Italy（意大利米兰，米兰理工大学，航空航天科学与技术系）
  • ² Institut für Strömungsmechanik, Technische Universität Braunschweig, Braunschweig, Germany（德国不伦瑞克，不伦瑞克工业大学，流体力学研究所）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 139–165

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-062537

Abstract

In-flight ice accretion poses a major safety concern in aviation. It occurs due to the icing from clouds of supercooled water droplets, the accumulation of ice crystals at high altitudes, or snow buildup. Assessing the detrimental effects of ice accretion on aircraft performance and handling qualities is a complex, multidisciplinary task, as it requires modeling the dynamics of the dispersed phase (water droplets, ice crystals, and snowflakes), the particle impact dynamics and its interaction with the aircraft surfaces, the liquid film dynamics, the solidification process, and possibly ice shedding. The ice protection systems must also be modeled. The present review addresses the status of ice accretion models and simulation tools. The intrinsically stochastic nature of ice accretion, combined with substantial operational and modeling uncertainties, makes it challenging to validate these tools against experimental observations and use simulation as a reliable means of compliance for certification in icing conditions, especially for innovative aircraft configurations such as wing-body and advanced urban air mobility vehicles.

摘要（中文）

飞行中积冰是航空领域的一个重大安全隐患。它由过冷水滴云团、高海拔冰晶积聚或积雪引起。评估积冰对飞机性能和操纵品质的不利影响是一项复杂的多学科任务，需要对分散相（水滴、冰晶和雪花）动力学、颗粒撞击动力学及其与飞机表面的相互作用、液膜动力学、凝固过程以及可能的脱冰进行建模。冰防护系统也必须被建模。本综述讨论了积冰模型和模拟工具的现状。积冰本质上具有随机性，加上大量的操作和建模不确定性，使得验证这些工具与实验观测的一致性、将模拟作为结冰条件下适航认证的可靠手段变得具有挑战性，特别是对于翼身融合和先进城市空中交通飞行器等创新构型飞机。

8. Flexible Fibers in Turbulence / 湍流中的柔性纤维

Authors / 作者: Cristian Marchioli¹, Marco Edoardo Rosti², Gautier Verhille³

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Udine, Udine, Italy（意大利乌迪内，乌迪内大学，工程与建筑系）
  • ² Complex Fluids and Flows Unit, Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology Graduate University, Okinawa, Japan（日本冲绳，冲绳科学技术大学院大学，复杂流体与流动单元）
  • ³ Aix Marseille Université, CNRS, Centrale Méditerranée, IRPHE, Marseille, France（法国马赛，艾克斯-马赛大学，法国国家科学研究中心，地中海中央理工，IRPHE研究所）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 167–192

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-050451

Abstract

In this article, we review recent progress in the fundamental understanding of the motion of flexible fibers in a turbulent flow, made through multiscale experiments and simulations. Emphasis is given to problems involving flexible fibers that can be conveniently described with discrete and continuum models closely related to the slender body theory. Current state-of-the-art measurement and simulation methods, including optical techniques, Euler–Lagrange approaches for tracking large swarms of fibers, and recent methodologies for simulating finite-size fibers, are discussed. The capabilities of simulations and experiments are surveyed in connection with the current physical understanding of how flexible fibers interact with the full spectrum of length scales and timescales of turbulence. We review the phenomenological and statistical features of fiber dispersion and spatial distribution. We also discuss the relevant aspects of fiber rotation and deformation, highlighting their connection with mechanisms such as fragmentation and turbulence modulation, which are known to exhibit peculiar features in the case of flexible fibers. We conclude our analysis by providing an outlook on future research direction paths, open methodological issues, and expected advances, in particular those associated with the study of flexible particles in a broader sense.

摘要（中文）

本文综述了通过多尺度实验和模拟在理解湍流中柔性纤维运动方面的最新进展。重点讨论了可用与细长体理论密切相关的离散和连续模型方便描述的柔性纤维问题。文章讨论了当前最先进的测量和模拟方法，包括光学技术、用于追踪大量纤维群的欧拉-拉格朗日方法，以及模拟有限尺寸纤维的最新方法论。综述了模拟和实验在与当前对柔性纤维如何与湍流全尺度（长度和时间尺度）相互作用的物理理解相关方面的能力。我们回顾了纤维弥散和空间分布的现象学和统计特征。还讨论了纤维旋转和变形的相关方面，强调了它们与破碎和湍流调制等机制的联系——这些机制在柔性纤维情况下表现出独特的特征。最后，我们展望了未来研究方向、开放的方法论问题以及预期进展，特别是与更广义的柔性粒子研究相关的进展。

9. Urban Fluid Mechanics, Resilience, and Sustainability / 城市流体力学、韧性与可持续性

Authors / 作者: Catherine Gorlé¹

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Stanford University, Stanford, California, USA（美国加利福尼亚州斯坦福，斯坦福大学，土木与环境工程系）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 193–219

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-100224-111114

Abstract

This review first examines how urban wind flow impacts the sustainability and resilience of cities and identifies the three main challenges in predictive modeling of urban flows: the complexity of the flow physics, the variability and uncertainty in the flow conditions, and the diversity and multiscale nature of urban geometries. To review the complexity of the flow physics, the typical flow patterns observed in canonical urban flows are summarized, and related modeling challenges and opportunities in both wind tunnel experiments and simulations are highlighted. Next, opportunities to predict realistic urban flows by addressing the other challenges are explored through the lens of a modeling framework with uncertainty quantification. The important role of field measurements, supporting the more accurate characterization of uncertainties in the flow conditions, as well as enabling validation with real-world data, is emphasized. The review concludes with two specific examples that demonstrate how integrated use of field measurements and computational models can improve the understanding and modeling of real urban flows to ultimately support sustainable development goals for urban areas.

摘要（中文）

本综述首先考察了城市风场如何影响城市的可持续性和韧性，并指出了城市流动预测建模的三个主要挑战：流动物理的复杂性、流动条件的变异性和不确定性，以及城市几何形状的多样性和多尺度性质。为回顾流动物理的复杂性，文章总结了典型城市流动中观察到的典型流动模式，并强调了风洞实验和模拟中相关的建模挑战与机遇。接下来，通过带有不确定性量化的建模框架，探索了通过解决其他挑战来预测真实城市流动的途径。文章强调了现场测量的重要作用——它支持更准确地表征流动条件中的不确定性，同时实现与真实世界数据的验证。综述以两个具体示例结尾，展示了现场测量和计算模型的综合使用如何增进对真实城市流动的理解和建模，最终支持城市地区的可持续发展目标。

10. Filtration in Pore Networks / 孔隙网络中的过滤

Authors / 作者: Linda J. Cummings¹, Binan Gu², Lou Kondic¹

Affiliations / 单位:

  • ¹ Department of Mathematical Sciences, New Jersey Institute of Technology, Newark, New Jersey, USA（美国新泽西州纽瓦克，新泽西理工学院，数学科学系）
  • ² Department of Mathematical Sciences, Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Worcester, Massachusetts, USA（美国马萨诸塞州伍斯特，伍斯特理工学院，数学科学系）

Pages / 页码: Vol. 58, pp. 221–244

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-054759

Abstract

In liquid filtration, a particulate-laden feed solution is passed through a porous material (the filter), often a membrane, designed to capture the particulate matter. Although some research progress has been made in understanding how fires spread on grasslands, several aspects of fire behavior within the forest canopy environment are still not well-understood. This review is an attempt to organize the fluid mechanics of the mass, momentum, and energy transfer during wildland fire events through the lens of vegetation canopy turbulence. The structure, organization, and progress of the flame front and the buoyant plume through the canopy are shown to be intricately related to the coherent structures associated with fire–vegetation–atmosphere interaction, and potential future research directions are identified.

DOI: 10.1146/annurev-fluid-112723-053105

Abstract

Decarbonization of buildings is one of the main challenges for the energy transition. In particular, the provision of heating, cooling, and ventilation to maintain a comfortable and healthy interior environment can be very energy intensive. Three approaches to help with the decarbonization of buildings are (a) upgrading the building envelope, especially the insulation, to reduce heat flow to or from the exterior; (b) improving the efficiency of the heating or cooling system, including the design and operation of ventilation flows; and (c) decarbonization of the heating and cooling systems, typically through electrification using heat pumps, and possibly the development of heat networks and interseasonal heat storage. This review touches on different elements of these challenges, mainly those related to ventilation, exploring some of the complexities of the fluid mechanics involved.
